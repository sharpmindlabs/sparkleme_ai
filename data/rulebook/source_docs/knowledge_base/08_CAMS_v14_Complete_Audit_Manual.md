# 08_CAMS_v14_Complete_Audit_Manual

CAMS AI KNOWLEDGE BASE v14.0  COMPLETE AUDIT MANUAL  AI AUDIT EXECUTION SPECIFICATION  PURPOSE  This document defines the complete CAMS audit framework.  Audits exist to prevent:  -   visual shortcuts,  -   palette bias,  -   incorrect weighting,  -   confusion between appearance and physiology.  Each audit defines:  -   purpose,  -   trigger,  -   required questions,  -   evidence,  -   positive indicators,  -   negative indicators,  -   failure conditions,  -   decision impact.  Excluded:
-   client cases  -   client images  -   historical outcomes  -   unavailable calibration libraries  ==================================================  AUDIT STRUCTURE  ==================================================  Every audit must answer:  1. Why is this audit required?  2. What triggered it?  3. What evidence must be examined?  4. What conclusions are valid?  5. What conclusions are invalid?  6. How does it affect the decision?  ==================================================  AUDIT 001   —   INDEPENDENT PHYSIOLOGY AUDIT  ==================================================  Purpose:  Determine whether a palette creates genuine improvement.  Trigger:  Every close comparison.
Questions:  Does the face improve physiologically?  Does the palette reduce unhealthy effects?  Does improvement remain when colour impact is separated?  Evidence:  -   facial details  -   skin colour  -   skin consistency  -   vitality  -   harmony  Positive:  -   healthier complexion  -   reduced unhealthy shadow  -   improved continuity  Negative:  -   visual impact only  -   stronger colour without facial benefit  Decision Impact:
Physiological improvement receives priority.  ==================================================  AUDIT 002   —   EFFECT SOURCE SEPARATION AUDIT  ==================================================  Purpose:  Separate real improvement from palette - created effects.  Trigger:  Any claimed advantage.  Classify:  A. Physiological improvement  B. Palette - created effect  C. Uncertain  Positive:  Advantage improves the person.  Negative:  Advantage only changes visual intensity.  Decision Impact:
Only genuine improvement should strongly influence ranking.  ==================================================  AUDIT 003   —   NATURAL LUMINOSITY AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Spring, Light, Bright, or luminosity boundaries.  Questions:  Does the face appear naturally open?  Is there natural sheen?  Are eyes more alive?  Is colour activity healthy?  Positive:  -   vitality  -   openness  -   healthy colour  Negative:  -   artificial brightness  -   surface intensity only
Decision Impact:  Luminosity is valid only when physiological.  ==================================================  AUDIT 004   —   GREY VEIL AND SUPPRESSION AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Summer, Muted, or softer palette comparisons.  Questions:  Is softness harmony?  Or is information being reduced?  Positive:  -   preserved identity  -   healthy softness  Negative:  -   grey suppression  -   flattened face  -   reduced vitality  Decision Impact:
Suppression cannot be mistaken for improvement.  ==================================================  AUDIT 005   —   AUTHENTIC PIGMENTATION SOURCE AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Warm, Autumn, and pigmentation decisions.  Questions:  Is warmth coming from healthy pigmentation?  Or from palette - induced colour pressure?  Evaluate:  -   believable flesh colour  -   redness  -   yellow/orange influence  -   reflected colour  Decision Impact:  Authentic pigmentation is valid.  Artificial colour pressure is not.  ==================================================
AUDIT 006   —   SHADOW REDUCTION AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Close physiology boundaries.  Questions:  Are unhealthy shadows reduced?  Is facial anatomy preserved?  Positive:  -   healthier modelling  -   clearer facial information  Negative:  -   loss of structure  -   artificial flattening  ==================================================  AUDIT 007   —   HEALTHY MODELLING VS SHADOW AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  High - definition or contrast comparisons.
Question:  Is definition supporting the face?  Or adding unhealthy darkness?  Positive:  -   natural structure  Negative:  -   under - eye burden  -   harshness  -   heaviness  ==================================================  AUDIT 008   —   WARMTH SOURCE AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Spring vs Autumn or warm palette decisions.  Questions:  Is warmth authentic?  Does warmth improve harmony?
Positive:  -   believable warmth  -   healthy pigmentation  Negative:  -   redness  -   orange pressure  -   colour dominance  ==================================================  AUDIT 009   —   VITALITY VS GROUNDING AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Spring vs Autumn and depth conflicts.  Separate:  Vitality:  -   openness  -   animation  -   colour activity  Grounding:  -   richness  -   depth  -   structure
Decision:  Grounding cannot automatically beat vitality.  ==================================================  AUDIT 010   —   BIOLOGICAL ANIMATION AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Bright competitions.  Questions:  Does the face become naturally more alive?  Do eyes animate?  Does person - first focus improve?  Positive:  -   genuine animation  Negative:  -   brightness only  ==================================================  AUDIT 011   —   EYE HIERARCHY SEPARATION AUDIT
==================================================  Trigger:  Winter, Bright, contrast competitions.  Separate:  Eye authority  from  Overall physiological harmony.  Decision:  Eyes matter but cannot override complexion decline.  ==================================================  AUDIT 012   —   PRIMARY PHYSIOLOGY TIE VERIFICATION  ==================================================  Trigger:  Close final decisions.  Required order:  1. Skin health  2. Facial details
3. Continuity  4. Vitality  5. Secondary effects  Do not move to secondary factors prematurely.  ==================================================  AUDIT 013   —   CUMULATIVE PHYSIOLOGY MAGNITUDE AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Repeated close comparisons.  Evaluate:  -   size of improvement  -   consistency  -   repeatability  Do not decide by one dramatic feature.  ==================================================  AUDIT 014   —   HOME PALETTE IDENTITY PRESERVATION AUDIT  ==================================================  Trigger:  Bridge palette challenges.
Question:  Does the bridge improve physiology while preserving identity?  Reject:  -   improvement that changes the person's natural harmony.  ==================================================  AUDIT 015   —   BRIDGE PALETTE IDENTITY RESOLUTION RULE  ==================================================  Trigger:  True versus bridge close decisions.  Evaluate:  -   physiology  -   identity preservation  -   harmony  Do not default to True.  Do not default to bridge.  ==================================================  AUDIT 016   —   FLOW VALIDATION AUDITS  ==================================================
Required for Flow boundaries:  True vs Bridge comparisons  Bright vs Cool:  Animation versus preservation.  Bright vs Deep:  Vitality versus heaviness.  Cool vs Muted:  Physiology versus suppression.  Light boundaries:  Softness versus genuine improvement.  Muted boundaries:  Harmony versus reduced information.  Deep boundaries:  Structure versus drag.  ==================================================  FINAL AUDIT REQUIREMENT  ==================================================
