CAMS AI KNOWLEDGE BASE v3.0  FULL RULE ENCYCLOPEDIA  AI TRAINING TRANSFER DOCUMENT  Purpose:  This document expands the CAMS methodology into a rule encyclopedia for training another AI  system.  It contains:  -   CAMS workflow rules  -   seven CAMS checks  -   complete audit framework  -   boundary rules  -   bridge validations  -   Flow comparison safeguards  -   anti - bias protections  -   finalization rules  Excluded:  -   client cases  -   client images  -   historical outcomes  -   unavailable calibration libraries  ==================================================  PART 1   —   NON - NEGOTIABLE CAMS OPERATING RULES
==================================================  The AI must:  -   Treat every case as completely new.  -   Analyse only approved drape evidence.  -   Ignore previous conclusions.  -   Ignore expected answers.  -   Ignore highlighted answers.  -   Ignore prior case outcomes.  -   Never use hair colour or Fitzpatrick unless explicitly provided.  No palette is automatically correct.  True palettes are candidates, not winners.  ==================================================  PART 2   —   CAMS DECISION FRAMEWORK  ==================================================  Decision hierarchy:  1. Eyes  2. Person  3. Colour  Decision priority:
1. Physiological improvement.  2. Eye vitality and authority.  3. Person - first harmony.  4. Colour relationship.  ==================================================  PART 3   —   SEVEN CAMS CHECKS  ==================================================  1. FACIAL DETAILS  Audit:  -   under - eye shadow  -   eye socket effects  -   cheek hollows  -   mouth corners  -   nasolabial areas  -   jaw effects  Separate:  Healthy modelling  from  unhealthy shadow.  2. SKIN COLOUR
Check:  -   believable flesh colour  -   healthy colour presence  -   grey veil  -   white patches  -   yellow/sallow cast  -   redness pressure  3. SKIN CONSISTENCY  Check:  -   whole - face pigmentation continuity.  Do not confuse:  smoothness with improvement.  4. JAWLINE  Check:  -   lift  -   support  -   heaviness  -   drag  5. EYES  Check:
-   authority  -   animation  -   vitality  6. FOCUS  Mandatory:  Eyes → Person → Colour  7. BALANCE/HARMONY  Check:  -   integration  -   palette support  -   whether colour overwhelms.  ==================================================  PART 4   —   COMPLETE AUDIT ENCYCLOPEDIA  ==================================================  INDEPENDENT PHYSIOLOGY AUDIT  Purpose:  Prevent visual effects from replacing real improvement.  Trigger:  Every close comparison.
Check:  -   facial health  -   shadow burden  -   continuity  -   believable colour.  --------------------------------------------------  EFFECT SOURCE SEPARATION AUDIT  Classify every advantage:  A. Genuine physiological improvement.  B. Palette - created visual effect.  C. Uncertain.  --------------------------------------------------  NATURAL LUMINOSITY AUDIT  Trigger:  Spring, Light, Brightness, luminosity disputes.  Evaluate:  -   facial openness  -   natural sheen
-   eye vitality  -   healthy colour activity.  --------------------------------------------------  GREY VEIL AUDIT  Trigger:  Cool, Summer, Muted, softer comparisons.  Check:  -   grey suppression  -   loss of facial information  -   washed appearance.  --------------------------------------------------  AUTHENTIC PIGMENTATION SOURCE AUDIT  Trigger:  Warm, Autumn, Spring, colour - pressure comparisons.  Separate:  -   authentic pigmentation  -   reflected colour  -   redness  -   yellow/orange cast.
--------------------------------------------------  SHADOW REDUCTION AUDIT  Trigger:  close physiology boundaries.  Check:  -   reduced unhealthy shadow  -   preserved anatomy  -   improved facial information.  --------------------------------------------------  WARMTH SOURCE AUDIT  Trigger:  Spring vs Autumn or warm palette decisions.  Determine:  Authentic warmth  versus  added colour concentration.  --------------------------------------------------
VITALITY VS GROUNDING AUDIT  Trigger:  Spring/Autumn boundaries.  Separate:  Vitality:  -   openness  -   animation  -   colour activity  Grounding:  -   depth  -   richness  -   structure  Do not confuse grounding with improvement.  --------------------------------------------------  EYE HIERARCHY SEPARATION AUDIT  Trigger:  Winter, Bright, contrast competitions.  Separate:  -   eye authority
-   biological animation  -   colour contrast.  --------------------------------------------------  BIOLOGICAL ANIMATION AUDIT  Trigger:  Bright competitions.  Bright may win when:  -   eyes animate naturally,  -   face becomes alive,  -   person - first hierarchy improves.  Brightness alone is insufficient.  --------------------------------------------------  PRIMARY PHYSIOLOGY TIE VERIFICATION  Trigger:  close decisions.  Check:  -   skin health first  -   facial continuity
-   shadow burden  Do not move to secondary factors prematurely.  --------------------------------------------------  CUMULATIVE PHYSIOLOGY MAGNITUDE AUDIT  Trigger:  repeated close comparisons.  Evaluate:  -   size of improvement,  -   consistency,  -   repeatability.  ==================================================  PART 5   —   FLOW AUDITS AND CROSS CHECKS  ==================================================  TRUE WINTER VS COOL  Audit:  Moderation vs preservation.  Cool cannot win only because it is:  -   cleaner
-   calmer  -   refined.  Require physiological benefit.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE WINTER VS BRIGHT  Audit:  Biological Animation Audit.  Check:  -   vitality  -   eye animation  -   person - first hierarchy.  Winter cannot win only through authority.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE WINTER VS DEEP  Audit:  Deep Preservation Audit.  Check:
-   structure without heaviness.  -   no white/grey patching.  -   no shadow burden.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE SUMMER VS COOL  Audit:  Structural Definition vs Physiology.  Definition is neutral until proven beneficial.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE SUMMER VS LIGHT  Audit:  Light Boundary Physiology Audit.  Light must show:  -   reduced shadows  -   improved continuity  -   genuine benefit.  Softness alone is insufficient.
--------------------------------------------------  TRUE SUMMER VS MUTED  Audit:  Suppression Boundary Audit.  Muted cannot win only by reducing colour.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE SPRING VS WARM  Audit:  Authentic Warmth Audit.  Warm must improve physiology, not simply add warmth.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE SPRING VS LIGHT  Audit:  Light Boundary Physiology Audit.  Reject Light only if it:  -   greys,
-   flattens,  -   suppresses.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE SPRING VS BRIGHT  Audit:  Physiology Separation Audit.  Bright may win if:  -   animation improves,  -   vitality improves.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE AUTUMN VS WARM  Audit:  Independent Improvement Audit.  Warm must provide more than visible warmth.  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE AUTUMN VS MUTED
Audits:  -   Authentic Pigmentation Magnitude Audit  -   Identity Preservation Audit  -   Colour Concentration Audit  --------------------------------------------------  TRUE AUTUMN VS DEEP  Audit:  Authentic Structure Audit.  Deep must avoid:  -   heaviness  -   drag  -   loss of vitality.  ==================================================  PART 6   —   HOME SEASON BRIDGE VALIDATIONS  ==================================================  Winter - Summer - Cool:  Evaluate independently.
