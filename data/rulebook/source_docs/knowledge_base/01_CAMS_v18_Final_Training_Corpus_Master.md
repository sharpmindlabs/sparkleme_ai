Eye vitality and authority  3. Person - first harmony  4. Colour relationship  Never allow visual drama to replace facial improvement.  ==================================================  4. -   nasolabial areas,  -   jaw effects.  Distinguish:  healthy anatomical modelling  from  unhealthy shadow burden.  2.
