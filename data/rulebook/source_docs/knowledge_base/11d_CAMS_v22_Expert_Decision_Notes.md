# 11d_CAMS_v22_Expert_Decision_Notes

CAMS AI KNOWLEDGE BASE v22.0  EXPERT DECISION NOTES  AI ADVANCED JUDGMENT SPECIFICATION  PURPOSE  This document captures expert - level CAMS interpretation principles.  It defines how the AI should reason when multiple palettes appear visually acceptable.  Excluded:  -   client cases  -   client images  -   historical outcomes  -   unavailable calibration examples  ==================================================  1. EXPERT DECISION FRAMEWORK  ==================================================  CAMS analysis is not a search for the most impressive colour.  The correct question is:  Which palette allows the person to look most harmonious and healthy?
The AI must evaluate:  -   facial response,  -   physiology,  -   vitality,  -   identity preservation.  The palette is evaluated by its effect on the person.  ==================================================  2. DEFINITION VS SHADOW  ==================================================  Expert rule:  More definition is not automatically better.  The AI must ask:  Is anatomy becoming clearer?  OR  Is additional darkness creating the appearance of definition?  Positive:  -   healthy modelling
-   preserved facial information  -   improved structure  Negative:  -   harsh shadow  -   heaviness  -   reduced vitality  Required audit:  Healthy Modelling vs Shadow Audit.  ==================================================  3. SMOOTHNESS VS SUPPRESSION  ==================================================  Expert rule:  A smoother appearance does not automatically mean improvement.  The AI must determine the source.  Possible causes:  A. Genuine harmony  B. Reduced facial information
C. Suppression of natural colour activity  Required audit:  Grey Veil / Suppression Audit.  ==================================================  4. BRIGHTNESS VS VITALITY  ==================================================  Expert rule:  Brightness does not automatically equal life.  The AI must separate:  Brightness:  -   increased visual intensity  from  Vitality:  -   eye animation  -   facial openness  -   healthy colour activity  Required audit:  Natural Luminosity Audit and Biological Animation Audit.
==================================================  5. WARMTH VS HEALTHY PIGMENTATION  ==================================================  Expert rule:  Warmth does not automatically equal healthy skin response.  The AI must determine whether warmth is:  Authentic:  -   believable pigmentation  -   integrated colour  or  Palette - created:  -   redness  -   orange pressure  -   artificial warmth  Required audit:  Authentic Pigmentation Source Audit.  ==================================================  6. DEPTH VS GROUNDING
==================================================  Expert rule:  Depth does not automatically equal harmony.  The AI must separate:  Grounding:  -   support  -   richness  -   stability  from:  Heaviness:  -   drag  -   reduced vitality  -   loss of openness  Required audit:  Vitality vs Grounding Audit.  ==================================================  7. EXPERT BOUNDARY OBSERVATIONS  ==================================================
The AI must remember:  The strongest palette is not always the best palette.  The quietest palette is not always the most harmonious.  The brightest palette is not always the most alive.  The deepest palette is not always the most grounded.  The warmest palette is not always the healthiest.  The smoothest palette is not always the most flattering.  ==================================================  8. CLOSE DECISION FRAMEWORK  ==================================================  When two palettes are close:  Step 1:  Avoid assumptions.  Step 2:  Increase audit depth.  Step 3:
Prioritize primary physiology.  Step 4:  Check eye/person hierarchy.  Step 5:  Confirm identity preservation.  Step 6:  Use cumulative evidence.  Do not force a decision from a single feature.  ==================================================  9. EXPERT CAMS QUESTIONS  ==================================================  Before finalizing ask:  Is the skin healthier?  Are shadows reduced or merely changed?  Are eyes more alive?  Does the person remain dominant?
Is the effect authentic?  Would this still win if palette labels were hidden?  Did the palette improve the person or only create impact?  ==================================================  10. ADVANCED ILLUSION PREVENTION  ==================================================  Prevent:  Contrast illusion:  Strong contrast mistaken for harmony.  Refinement illusion:  Clean appearance mistaken for improvement.  Smoothness illusion:  Evenness mistaken for health.  Warmth illusion:  Colour intensity mistaken for pigmentation.  Richness illusion:  Depth mistaken for compatibility.
Brightness illusion:  Intensity mistaken for vitality.  ==================================================  11. EXPERT DECISION LANGUAGE  ==================================================  Preferred CAMS language:  -   improves facial integration  -   reduces unnecessary shadow  -   preserves facial information  -   creates healthy colour activity  -   improves natural luminosity  -   maintains person - first harmony  Avoid unsupported language:  -   simply looks better  -   stronger colour wins  -   brighter is better  -   warmer is better  -   deeper is better  ==================================================  TRANSFER AUDIT
